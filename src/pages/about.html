Our Story

Lawn Craft started the way most good things do — out of genuine frustration. After struggling to find a lawn care provider that actually showed up on time, communicated clearly, and took real pride in the work, we decided to build that service ourselves. What began as a small operation in Nairobi's Kitisuru area has grown into a trusted name across the city and beyond.

We are a hands-on team. When we take on a property, we treat it the same way we would treat our own — paying attention to the details that others overlook, showing up when we say we will, and always leaving the space better than we found it. That personal commitment is what keeps our clients coming back and recommending us to their neighbours.
